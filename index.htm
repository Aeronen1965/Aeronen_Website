My biography:

I am a Senior Principal Power Electronics Engineer with over 39 years of experience leading the research, design and commercialisation of advanced power conversion technologies.

My career has combined technology innovation with successful product development, taking ideas from concept through architecture, detailed design, verification and manufacture into commercially successful products. During the last decade I have led advanced research focused on next-generation power conversion while maintaining a strong focus on practical engineering and product delivery.

I have held senior technical and research positions at Dyson Technology, the Compound Semiconductor Applications Catapult and Emerson Control Techniques, contributing to products ranging from precision power supplies and industrial motor drives to megawatt-scale power conversion systems and innovative cost sensitive consumer products.

My expertise includes power converter architectures, motor drives, power supplies, wide-bandgap semiconductors (SiC and GaN), EMC and signal integrity, magnetics, simulation and system optimisation. I have extensive experience in developing technologies that balance performance, efficiency, reliability, manufacturability and cost.

I am the inventor or co-inventor of ten patent families, including three as sole inventor, reflecting a long track record of technical innovation and commercially relevant engineering solutions.

I enjoy solving technically challenging problems, mentoring engineers and helping organisations make informed technology and engineering decisions based on both deep theoretical understanding and extensive practical experience.
